Three tools, one conversation

Everything lives inside the chat.

01 — OCR Scanner

Point, scan, understand.

Photograph a medicine strip or food label. HealthWise pulls the product name, composition, expiry date and usage instructions straight out of the image, then explains them in the chat.

  • Product name & composition
  • Expiry date detection
  • Plain-language usage notes

02 — Community

Shared experience, sorted.

Report a reaction, review a product, or read what others found. Feedback is grouped by medicine so patterns in side effects or quality issues surface quickly.

  • Report & discuss medicines
  • Quality and side-effect reviews
  • Threaded, searchable history

03 — History Checker

One record, always current.

Upload prescriptions or connect your ABHA (Ayushman Bharat Health Account) to keep verified health data synced and accessible right inside the assistant.

  • Prescription uploads
  • ABHA account linking
  • Full conversation export to PDF
